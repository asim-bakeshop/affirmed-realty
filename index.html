First-Time Home Buyer Programs

Yes, You Can Afford This.

Down payments and closing costs stop more first-time buyers than anything else. There are more ways around that wall than most people ever hear about — and we make it our job to know them.

  • Low down payment loans — FHA financing from as little as 3.5% down
  • VA loans for eligible veterans and active service members
  • State and local down payment assistance programs
  • Grants and closing-cost assistance for qualified buyers

Program availability and eligibility vary by lender and change over time — we'll help you find what actually fits your situation, and connect you with lenders who specialize in first-time buyers.
